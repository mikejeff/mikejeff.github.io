Card

Cards are a flexible way to group related content into a single, scannable unit. They work well for products, articles, profiles, and other content that benefits from a visual container. Depending on the use case, a card may include text, images, links, or actions.

Text-only card

Not every card needs an image. A text-only card is a good choice when the content itself is the focus and supporting media doesn't add much value. This keeps the interface lightweight while still providing a clear visual boundary around the content.

Image card

Images can help users quickly understand what a card represents. Adding media is especially useful for products, articles, destinations, or any content where a visual preview provides meaningful context. The image should support the content, not compete with it.

Image card with link

Cards often serve as a gateway to more detailed information. In this variation, the heading remains the semantic link while the entire card becomes a larger click target. This improves usability without sacrificing accessibility or discoverability.

Card Scroller

When several related cards need to be presented in a limited amount of space, a horizontal card scroller can be an effective solution. It allows users to browse a collection without overwhelming the page while maintaining a consistent card layout.
