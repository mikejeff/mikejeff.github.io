UX Workbench

Welcome to my workbench. This is where I build user interface components, patterns, and the foundations of a design system in real time. Everything here is evolving in public, which means most of it is in progress unless noted otherwise.

I design in the browser first. It’s the fastest way for me to test ideas, refine interactions, and make decisions in the medium the work actually lives in. It keeps the feedback loop tight, cuts down on translation between design tools and code, and makes it easier to focus on how something behaves instead of how polished the mockup looks. Or, to paraphrase Brad Frost: you can’t make toast with a picture of a toaster.

This workbench is where I figure out what holds up. Some of that means building components. Some of it means testing patterns, naming things, breaking systems apart, and putting them back together in ways that are easier to use and easier to maintain. Over time, this becomes a working system shaped by real constraints, iteration, and use.

I’m a developer with strong design instincts, and most of my thinking lives in the overlap between the two. This is where I work through that in public. If you’d like the broader version, my main site has a little more about me and the occasional post when I have something worth saying.
